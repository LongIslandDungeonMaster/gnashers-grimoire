# Gnasher's Grimoire

A Dungeon Master's screen for in-person D&D that runs from one file. Download it, open it in a browser, and it works with the wifi off. No account, no install, no subscription, and nothing you put in it leaves your computer.

**Download:** the latest build is on the [Releases](https://github.com/LongIslandDungeonMaster/gnashers-grimoire/releases) page as `gnashers-grimoire.html`. It is the same file offered at [longislanddungeonmaster.com/grimoire](https://longislanddungeonmaster.com/grimoire) and on [itch.io](https://longislanddungeonmaster.itch.io/gnashers-grimoire).

## What it does

- The free SRD 5.2.1 is already inside: monsters, spells, items and rules, nothing to import.
- Press Space, type three letters, and any table, monster or NPC from any book you own floats over the page you are running and rolls in place.
- The War Table musters an encounter from your party and any bestiary you own, rolls initiative, and tracks hit points and conditions.
- The War Board runs the fight on your own map images: a grid, fog you paint back, tokens, dynamic lighting, and video maps.
- Drop in your own PDFs, Word and Excel files, Markdown notes, CSV tables, or a whole Obsidian vault, and they join the search.
- Gnasher's Satchel, the free player companion, pairs to the table with a code. It lives at [longislanddungeonmaster.com/satchel](https://longislanddungeonmaster.com/satchel).

## How to run it

1. Download `gnashers-grimoire.html`.
2. Open it in a current browser.
3. That is all. Keep the file anywhere, a USB stick included, and it travels with your binder.

## What it is not

It is not a virtual tabletop for remote play, it does not import Roll20 or Foundry files, and it has no server. Your binder lives in your browser's storage and in any folder you point it at.
